---
title: Getting Started
description: How to sign in, submit your first bot, server, or pack, and what happens after you submit.
---

Omniplex lists three kinds of things: **bots**, **servers**, and **packs**
(curated collections of bots and servers). This guide covers signing in and
submitting each one.

## 1. Sign in

Use the "Sign in" button in the header to authenticate with Discord. You
need to be signed in before you can submit anything, vote, or manage your
dashboard.

## 2. Submit a bot

Go to **Add a Bot**. You'll need:

- **Bot ID**, your bot's Discord user/application ID, found in the
  [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications).
- **Short description**, shown on bot cards.
- **Invite URL**, the OAuth2 invite link for your bot.
- Optionally: prefix, library, tags, and a long description (HTML
  supported) for the bot's full page.

Before submitting, read [Bot Rules](/docs/guides/rules/bots) and
[Page Rules](/docs/guides/rules/pages), bots are reviewed against both.

## 3. Submit a server

Go to **Add a Server**. You'll need a Discord invite link, a short
description, and a working invite. Omniplex checks whether the tracking bot
is already in the server and prompts you to add it if it isn't, it's
required for your listing to work properly, not optional. Read
[Servers → Listing Rules](/docs/guides/rules/servers) first.

## 4. Create a pack

Go to **Add a Pack**. Packs need a name, a unique URL, a short description,
and at least one bot or server, only entities you've already listed
yourself can be added to a pack you create. See
[Packs → Pack Rules](/docs/guides/rules/packs).

## What happens after you submit

New bots and servers start in a **pending** review state. A member of our
staff team reviews the submission against the relevant rules before it's
approved and made public. This isn't instant, please don't resubmit or open
a support ticket asking for a status update shortly after submitting.

Once your listing is live, you can start collecting votes (see
[Voting](/docs/guides/voting)), and if it qualifies, apply for
[Certification](/docs/guides/programs/certification) later on.
