---
title: Pack Rules
description: The rules that all packs on Omniplex must follow.
---

A pack is a curated collection of bots and/or servers, grouped together
under a single name, short description, and page. Keep in mind that all of
the rules below are just general guidelines, we may offer exemptions or
extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis.

1. A pack must contain at least one bot or server, and only entities you have submitted yourself can be added to a pack.
2. Packs should group entities around a genuine, coherent theme (e.g. a suite of related bots, or a community's bot and its server) rather than being an arbitrary or random grouping.
3. Pack names and short descriptions must follow the same content rules as [Page Rules](/docs/guides/rules/pages), no spam, no NSFW mentions, no misleading information.
4. Do not create multiple near-duplicate packs solely to increase your visibility or vote count, a pack should serve a real discovery purpose for users.
5. Every bot or server included in a pack must individually follow the relevant [Bot Rules](/docs/guides/rules/bots) or [Server Listing Rules](/docs/guides/rules/servers), a pack does not exempt any of its entities from those requirements.
6. Staff reserve the right to remove a pack, or remove individual entities from a pack, at any time if it stops meeting these rules.
