# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Omniplex to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. This code of conduct outlines what we expect from everyone who participates, whether that's in this repository, our Discord server, or any other space connected to the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- Be constructive. If you're pointing out a problem, try to suggest a direction for fixing it.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was new to this project once.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are just that, misunderstandings.
- Respect people's time. Keep discussions on topic and avoid unnecessary noise.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery, in public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and our Discord server. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces, for example when posting from an official account.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to **conduct@nodebyte.co.uk**. Include as much detail as you can: what happened, when, and who was involved. Reports are handled in confidence.

If the report involves a maintainer, it will be reviewed by someone not involved in the incident.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct, and will take action they consider appropriate in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. This can include a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from project spaces, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.

## Attribution

This code of conduct was written for Omniplex and adapted from common practices used across open source projects, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
