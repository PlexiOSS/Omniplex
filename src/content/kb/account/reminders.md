If you vote regularly for a specific bot or server, you don't have to remember the cooldown yourself Omniplex can remind you.

## Setting a reminder

On any bot or server page, next to the report button, there's a bell-style reminder toggle. Click it to turn reminders on for that listing. You'll need to be signed in.

Once set, you'll get an alert (and a push notification, if you have those enabled see [Alerts & Push Notifications](/kb/account/notifications)) when that listing's vote cooldown ends and you're free to vote again.

## Turning a reminder off

Click the same toggle again on the bot or server page to remove the reminder. There's no limit on how many bots or servers you can set reminders for.

## What you'll be reminded about

Reminders are tied to the listing's cooldown, not to a fixed schedule a premium bot with a 4-hour cooldown reminds you every 4 hours, a standard listing every 12 (or 6, on a double-vote weekend). See [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules) for the full cooldown breakdown.
