You can download what Omniplex stores about you, or delete your account, yourself from the **Security** tab of your [dashboard](/dashboard). Neither needs a support ticket.

## Downloading your data

Click **Download my data**. We prepare a JSON file covering your account, teams, votes, reviews, support tickets, alerts and reminders, and it's ready to save on the same page once it's done. It can take a little while, so keep the tab open.

It doesn't include shop purchase history for listings you own, because that's tracked against the listing rather than your account.

## Deleting your account

Click **Delete my account** and confirm. This permanently removes your account and everything tied to it that we can identify as yours. It can't be undone.

- Bots and packs you own **directly** (not through a team) are deleted with your account.
- Bots and servers owned by a **team** stay with the team. Servers always belong to a team, so they're never deleted this way. If you're the only owner of a team and want it to continue, give someone else the Owner permission first (see [Teams](/kb/listings/teams)).

Deletion runs in the background. Keep the tab open until it finishes; if it takes longer than usual, it may still complete after you close it.

## Questions

If something's missing from your export, or you need help with a deletion, [open a support ticket](/tickets).
