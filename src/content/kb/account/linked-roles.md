Linked roles let a Discord server give you a role because of a badge you've earned on Omniplex, such as Top Voter or Early Supporter. Discord checks your Omniplex connection, so nobody has to hand the role out manually.

## Connecting your account

1. Go to [Linked Roles](/linked-roles) and choose **Connect with Discord**.
2. Discord asks you to approve two things: your username, and permission for Omniplex to update your Omniplex connection. Nothing else.
3. You're sent back to Omniplex, which shows which badges servers can now see.

You can also start from Discord: in a server that uses an Omniplex linked role, open the server menu, choose **Linked Roles**, and pick **Infernoplex**, the Omniplex bot that powers linked roles.

Your connection stays up to date by itself. When you earn or lose a badge, your Discord roles follow within the hour.

## Which badges can be used

Up to five badges can be used as linked roles at a time, which is a limit Discord sets. The [Linked Roles](/linked-roles) page lists the current ones.

## Setting up a linked role in your server

Linked roles come from **Infernoplex**, the Omniplex bot that also lists servers, so it needs to be in your server first. If you've listed your server on Omniplex, it already is.

1. In **Server Settings**, open **Roles** and pick (or create) the role.
2. Open the role's **Links** tab and choose **Add requirement**.
3. Pick **Infernoplex**, then the badge members need.

Members who meet the requirement can claim the role from the server menu under **Linked Roles**.

## Removing the connection

In Discord, go to **User Settings**, then **Connections**, and remove the Omniplex connection. Servers stop seeing your badges straight away. If you connect again later, everything comes back.
