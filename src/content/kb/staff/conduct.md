This page describes what the platform's own design already holds staff to, not a separate rulebook layered on top of it. Human Resources can expand this with team-specific expectations, but everything below reflects how the system actually behaves today.

## Reporter and reviewer confidentiality

Who filed a report, and any private contact details on an entity, are visible only to staff **View Sensitive Data** gates the latter specifically. The [Moderation Transparency](/about/moderation) page publishes report counts by reason and status, and platform-wide safety stats never who filed what, or against whom specifically.

## Every action is logged

Every staff action taken through the panel or the staff bot is recorded and readable by anyone with **View Audit Logs**. There's no action that bypasses this trail.

## Dangerous permissions are marked, not hidden

A handful of permissions Force Remove Bots, Manage Premium, Manage Votes, Manage Staff Members, Manage Staff Roles, Manage Disciplinaries, and Manage CDN are flagged **Dangerous** because they're irreversible or hard to undo. Holding one is a signal to double-check before acting, not a reason to avoid the action when it's actually warranted.

## Reports don't self-execute

Resolving or dismissing a report never automatically removes, delists, or otherwise acts on the reported content that's always a separate, deliberate step through the **Actions** menu. See [Handling Reports & Tickets](/kb/staff/handling-reports-and-tickets) for the full workflow.

## Permissions are additive, not implicit

A position only grants what's explicitly listed against it Administrator is the one exception, since it implies everything. If a page or action isn't visible, the fix is a permission grant on the position (or an individual override), not working around it.
