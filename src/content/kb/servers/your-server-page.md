Most of your server page fills itself in from Discord through our tracking bot. Here's what each part shows and where it comes from.

## Members and online count

The sidebar shows your total members, and how many are online as a share of that total. The tracking bot updates both regularly. If you'd rather report counts from your own bot, see [Posting Stats](/kb/developers/posting-stats#servers).

## Boosts

If your server has any Nitro boosts, the sidebar shows its boost level and boost count. This is also synced by the tracking bot.

## Emojis and stickers

You can show your server's custom emojis and stickers on its page, where visitors can preview them. It's off by default. To turn it on, open your server in the [dashboard](/dashboard), go to **Details**, turn on **Show emojis and stickers on the listing**, and save. The tracking bot keeps the list in sync as you add or remove emojis.

## Votes, reviews and voters

- **Votes** count toward your server's ranking and reset monthly. See [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules).
- **Reviews** let members rate the server. As an owner you can reply with an owner response. See [Reviews](/kb/voting/reviews).
- **Voters** lists the people who voted this month, ranked.

## Sharing your server

- **Short link**: copy a short link to your page from the buttons under the description.
- **Embed**: the **Embed** button gives you a widget image for your website or GitHub README. See [Widgets](/kb/developers/widgets).
- **Vote reminders**: visitors can turn on a reminder so they're told when they can vote again.

## Managing it from Discord

With the tracking bot in your server, `/update`, `/delete` and `/leaderboard` let you manage the listing without opening the site. See [Listing a Server](/kb/listings/listing-a-server#managing-your-listing-from-discord).
