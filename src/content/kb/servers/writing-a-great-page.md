People join servers for the community, so your page should show what it's like to be a member, not just list channels.

## The short description

Shown on cards and in search, so say who the server is for and what happens there. "A friendly community for indie game devs, with weekly jams and feedback threads" beats "Best server, join now".

## The long description

Cover the things people want to know before joining:

- **What the server is about** and who it's for.
- **What members do**: events, game nights, help channels, showcases.
- **What makes it different** from other servers on the same topic.
- **The vibe**: how active it is, whether it's casual or focused, and any key rules.

Markdown and HTML both work, including headings, lists, links and images.

## Make it look the part

- **Add a banner** from your dashboard. See [Vanity URLs and Assets](/kb/listings/vanity-and-assets).
- **Pick accurate tags** so the server shows up in the right places.
- **Show off your emojis and stickers** by turning on **Show emojis and stickers on the listing** in your server's dashboard settings. See [Your Server Page](/kb/servers/your-server-page).
- **Add extra links** to your website or socials.

## Keep it honest

Don't inflate your activity, promise rewards for joining or voting, or describe content the server doesn't have. The [Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules) apply to everything on the page.
