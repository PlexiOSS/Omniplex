Like bots, every server is reviewed by staff before it's public on Omniplex. To add one, follow [Listing a Server](/kb/listings/listing-a-server).

## How review works

1. Your server joins the review queue as soon as you submit it.
2. A reviewer claims it and joins through your invite link to look around.
3. They approve or deny it, and you get a notification either way. A denial always comes with the reason.

Servers are reviewed in the order they were submitted, so please don't resubmit or ask staff to skip ahead.

## What reviewers check

- **The invite works and doesn't expire.** Use a permanent invite. If it has expired by the time a reviewer opens it, the server is denied.
- **The server matches its listing.** The description, tags and NSFW setting reflect what's actually there.
- **It's a real community.** Servers that only exist to farm votes, invites or rewards are denied.
- **NSFW content is age-gated.** If your server has NSFW channels, mark the listing NSFW and keep that content behind Discord's age-restricted channels.
- **It follows the rules.** See [Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules) and Discord's [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

## Tips for a smooth review

- **Keep the tracking bot in your server.** It's required to list a server, and it keeps your member counts and emojis up to date.
- **Make your rules and welcome channels easy to find.** Reviewers look at the first things a new member sees.
- **Write a description that explains the server**, not just its name. See [Writing a Great Server Page](/kb/servers/writing-a-great-page).

## If your server is denied

Read the reason in the notification, fix what it mentions, then submit the server again from [Add a Server](/servers/add). If you think the denial was a mistake, [open a support ticket](/tickets).
