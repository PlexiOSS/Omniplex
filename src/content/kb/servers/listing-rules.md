Keep in mind that all of the rules below are just general guidelines we may offer exemptions or extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis. These are the rules for servers *listed* on Omniplex. If you're looking for the rules of our own community server, see [Official Discord Server Rules](/kb/basics/server-rules). General conduct rules that apply beyond just server listings are covered in our [Acceptable Use policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

1. Your server must be public and have a working, permanent invite link at all times. Expired or one-time-use invites will get your listing delisted.
2. Abide by [Omniplex's Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).
3. Your listing's description and member counts should accurately represent the server don't misrepresent activity, purpose, or content to inflate rankings.
4. You may not mention NSFW content or use NSFW images (avatar, banner) unless the server is correctly tagged, and even then such content must stay behind Discord's own age-gating for NSFW channels.
5. Servers whose sole purpose is to farm votes, invites, or rewards without providing genuine community value will be denied or delisted.
6. Do not use deceptive invite-reward schemes, fake member/bot counts, or other manipulation to make the server appear more active or larger than it is.
7. Must not have the sole intent of impersonating another server or brand.
8. Use [Extra Links](/kb/bots/extra-links) and other listing features to point to real resources (website, socials, support) rather than unrelated or malicious content.
9. Staff reserve the right to delist a server at any time if it stops meeting these rules or becomes inactive/defunct.
