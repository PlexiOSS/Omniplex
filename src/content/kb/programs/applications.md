Staff team, dev team, partnership, and certification (bot **or** server) applications are all submitted the same way, through [Apply](/apps).

## Applying

1. Go to [Apply](/apps) and pick a position. Closed positions are shown but can't be applied to.
2. Fill out the position's questions. Short answers have a generous length limit; longer answers need at least 50 characters a one-line response won't pass validation.
3. Submit. You'll be able to track its status from your [dashboard](/dashboard)'s Applications tab.

## While it's pending

Most positions only let you have one pending application at a time some also enforce a cooldown before you can reapply after a decision. If you try to apply again too soon, the form will tell you how long to wait.

## After a decision

Once a staff member reviews your application, its status changes to **Approved** or **Denied** on your dashboard, along with any feedback they left. Denied applications for most positions can be reapplied to later check the position's own page for specifics.

## Position-specific requirements

The application form itself only covers the mechanics of applying. For what we actually look for in each position:

- [Certification Program](/kb/programs/certification) requirements and benefits for getting your bot or server certified.
- [Partner Requirements](/kb/programs/partnership) what we look for in a partnership.

Staff team and dev team positions don't have a separate requirements page yet the questions on the application itself cover what we're looking for.
