## Requirements

Keep in mind that all of the rules below are just general guidelines — we may offer exemptions or extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis (such as for high quality).

1. Has at least 30 unique views on the bot page.
2. Good long description with effective use of Markdown and/or HTML.
3. In 100 or more servers. This is really a must, due to verification.
4. Follows best practices for Discord bots.
5. Bot has been listed for at least 1 week.
6. Has an active, friendly, and helpful support server.
7. Has at least 50 votes.
8. 100% original code that is not cloned. If it is a fork, it should be heavily modified.
9. Should make use of site features such as Extra Links, etc.
10. Bonus: uses the bot widget on a website, command, or GitHub repository.

## Benefits

1. Certified badge for your bot card on our home page.
2. Certified bot role in our Discord Server (listed highest amongst other bots).
3. Featured on our main Discord Server.
4. Almost guaranteed chances of being featured in a home-page slot.

## Terms of Service

1. Abuse of the API or the voting system in an attempt to gain access to this program will result in a ban from the voting system, or a permanent banning of your bot and/or you from the service.
2. Any attempts to harm the Omniplex service may lead to the permanent banning of your bot and/or you from the service.
3. The staff team reserves the right to revoke your bot's certification at any time without warning.
