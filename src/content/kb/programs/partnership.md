We partner with Discord bots, servers, and businesses that align with Omniplex and can genuinely benefit our community not just anyone looking for a link exchange.

## What we look for

1. You're able to speak on behalf of the bot, server, or business you're applying for don't apply on behalf of something you don't represent.
2. A clear, specific reason for wanting to partner, rather than a generic "please partner with us."
3. A concrete way you intend to promote Omniplex in return for example a partner command, a link/badge on your website, or a shout-out in your own community.
4. Some evidence of reach or activity (member count, usage stats, or links to your bot/server/site) so we can evaluate the partnership fairly.

## How to apply

Partnership applications are reviewed by our staff team. There isn't an in-app application form yet reach out through our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/KBCRuBKrHe) and a staff member will walk you through the current process.

## Terms

- We reserve the right to deny or cancel any partnership application, or end an existing partnership, at any time.
- Partners are expected to keep their side of the agreement (promotion, links, etc.) up to date — a partnership that goes stale on your end may be revoked without warning.
