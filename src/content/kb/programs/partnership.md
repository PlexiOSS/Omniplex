We partner with Discord bots, servers, and businesses that align with Omniplex and can genuinely benefit our community not just anyone looking for a link exchange. We don't require a minimum size a small, genuinely active community is worth more to us than a large inactive one.

## What we look for

1. You're able to speak on behalf of the bot, server, or business you're applying for don't apply on behalf of something you don't represent.
2. A clear reason for wanting to partner doesn't need to be elaborate, just specific to you rather than generic.
3. A concrete way you intend to promote Omniplex in return for example a partner command, a link/badge on your website, or a shout-out in your own community.
4. Some evidence the thing you're partnering is real and active a member count, usage stats, or a link is enough. We're checking it's a genuine, active community/service, not gatekeeping by size.

## How to apply

Go to [Apply](/apps) and pick the Partners position see [How to Apply](/kb/programs/applications) for the mechanics of the application form itself. Partnership applications are reviewed by our staff team.

## Terms

- We reserve the right to deny or cancel any partnership application, or end an existing partnership, at any time.
- Partners are expected to keep their side of the agreement (promotion, links, etc.) up to date a partnership that goes stale on your end may be revoked without warning.
