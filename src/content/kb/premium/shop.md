Every vote a bot or server receives is worth something beyond the vote count itself it can be converted into credits and spent in the shop.

## Earning credits

Votes convert into credits at a tiered rate the first block of votes earns more per vote than later blocks, tapering off the more a listing accumulates. Go to [Shop](/shop), pick **Bot** or **Server**, then pick which one, and you'll see how many unredeemed votes it has and how many credits converting them would earn. Converting is a manual action a listing can rack up votes for a while before you decide to cash them in.

## Spending credits

The [Shop](/shop) lists items your credit balance can be spent on. What's available can change, but typically includes:

- **Bonus premium days** extends the premium period, the same effect as buying premium directly.
- **Priority Boost** bumps it toward the top of listing sort order for a limited time.
- **Featured Homepage Slot** puts it in the home page's Featured section for a limited time.
- **Supporter Badge** a permanent cosmetic badge on its card and page.
- **Vote Blitz** — halves its vote cooldown for a limited time, useful for a short push on vote count.

Each item shows its price in credits and how long its effect lasts, and which of bots/servers it applies to. You can only buy what your available balance covers.

## Purchase history

The Shop page shows a purchase history for whichever bot or server you have selected, so you can see what's been bought and when.

## Credits belong to the listing, not you

Vote credits are tracked per bot or server, not per user they're earned from that listing's votes and spent on that listing, regardless of which of your bots or servers you're currently viewing in the shop.
