## How voting works

You can vote for a bot or server once per cooldown period. Each vote counts as 1 vote per user by default.

## Cooldowns

- The standard cooldown between votes is **12 hours**.
- **Premium** bots and servers have a shorter cooldown of **4 hours**.
- On **weekends** (Friday through Sunday), non-premium bots, servers, packs, and teams get a **double-vote weekend bonus**: each vote counts for 2, and the cooldown is halved to **6 hours**.

If you try to vote again before your cooldown ends, you'll see how long you need to wait before you can vote again.

## Upvotes and downvotes

- **Bots** only support upvotes bots cannot be downvoted.
- **Servers, packs, and teams** support both upvotes and downvotes.

## Vote credits

Bots and servers support vote credits a mechanism for redeeming accumulated votes for perks. Availability depends on what the specific bot or server has opted into.

## Vote abuse

Using alternate accounts, vote-selling, vote-for-vote schemes, or any other form of vote manipulation is strictly prohibited. Accounts caught doing this can be **vote banned** a vote ban blocks you from casting any further votes and can be applied independently of a full account ban. If you believe you were vote banned by mistake, contact support.
