## How voting works

You can vote for a bot, server, team or pack once per cooldown period, and you need to be signed in to vote. Each vote counts as 1 by default. Only approved and certified listings can receive votes.

When you vote, your browser solves a short verification check first (you'll see "Verifying…" on the button for a moment). It runs automatically and doesn't ask you to do anything; it exists to make automated voting expensive.

## Cooldowns

- The standard cooldown between votes is **12 hours**.
- **Premium** bots and servers have a shorter cooldown of **4 hours**.
- On **weekends** (Friday through Sunday, **UTC**), non-premium bots, servers, packs, and teams get a **double-vote weekend bonus**: each vote counts for 2, and the cooldown is halved to **6 hours**. The boundary is UTC regardless of your own timezone, so the bonus may start or end a few hours earlier or later than local Friday/Sunday depending on where you are. Premium listings already have the shorter 4-hour cooldown and don't additionally get the weekend bonus.
- A listing with an active **Vote Blitz** (bought in the [Shop](/kb/premium/shop)) has its cooldown halved for as long as the blitz lasts.

If you try to vote again before your cooldown ends, you'll see how long you need to wait. You can also have Omniplex [remind you](/kb/account/reminders) when you're able to vote again.

## Upvotes and downvotes

Bots, servers, packs, and teams all support both upvotes and downvotes. A downvote asks you to confirm first, because it can't be removed or changed once it's submitted. Voting again before your cooldown ends is blocked regardless of direction, so you can't use a downvote to immediately reverse an upvote (or vice versa) within the same cooldown window.

## The monthly reset

Vote counts reset once a month, so every listing starts the month on an even footing and rankings reflect who's being voted for now rather than years ago. After a reset:

- A listing's displayed vote count starts again from 0.
- Your own vote history and the all-time voter leaderboard are kept.
- Votes that weren't converted into [vote credits](/kb/premium/shop) before the reset can't be converted afterwards, so redeem them first.

## The voter leaderboard

The [Voter Leaderboard](/leaderboard) ranks the most active voters, both **this month** (since the last reset) and **all time**. It counts upvotes for bots, servers, teams and packs. Votes that staff removed because of abuse, and votes from vote-banned or banned accounts, don't count. If you're signed in, the leaderboard and your profile both show your own rank.

## Vote credits

The votes a bot or server receives can be converted into credits and spent in the [Shop](/shop) on things like bonus premium days and priority placement. See [Vote Credits & the Shop](/kb/premium/shop) for the full breakdown.

## Vote abuse

Using alternate accounts, vote-selling, vote-for-vote schemes, or any other form of vote manipulation is strictly prohibited. Accounts caught doing this can be **vote banned**: a vote ban blocks you from casting any further votes, and it can be applied independently of a full account ban. Staff can also reset a listing's votes if they were gained through abuse. If you believe you were vote banned by mistake, [open a support ticket](/tickets).
