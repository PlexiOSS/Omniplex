For anything a staff member needs to personally look into your account, a payment, a listing, a bug, or anything else open a ticket rather than posting in Discord.

## Opening a ticket

1. Go to [Support Tickets](/tickets) and click **New Ticket**.
2. Pick a topic Billing & Payments, Account Issues, Bug Report, Report Abuse, or Other.
3. Give it a short subject line and describe what's going on in as much detail as you can. Include relevant IDs or links if you have them; it saves a round-trip.
4. Submit. A staff member will reply here it's not a live chat, so replies may take a bit.

## Replying

Open the ticket from your [tickets list](/tickets) and use the reply box at the bottom. You'll get notified (see [Alerts & Push Notifications](/kb/account/notifications)) when staff respond.

## Closing and reopening

You can close your own ticket at any time once it's resolved. A closed ticket can't receive new replies. If you need it reopened, ask in a new ticket or through Discord staff can reopen an existing one on request.

## Before you open one

If your question is about a specific program's requirements or how a feature works, check the rest of this Knowledge Base first a lot of common questions are already answered here without needing to wait on a reply.
