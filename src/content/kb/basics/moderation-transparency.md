Moderation on Omniplex isn't a black box. [Moderation Transparency](/about/moderation) publishes aggregate numbers about how reports and bot reviews are handled without exposing anyone's identity.

## What's published

- **Report counts** broken down by reason (license violation, ToS violation, spam, other) and status (open, under review, resolved, dismissed), plus an overall resolution rate.
- **Bot review pipeline** counts approved, certified, awaiting review, and denied bots platform-wide.

## What's not published

Individual reports, who filed them, and who they were filed against are never shown here that information is visible to staff only. This page only ever shows counts.

## Reporting something

Any bot, server, or pack has a "Report this" link near the bottom of its listing. Pick a reason and describe what's wrong staff review every report, and the outcome (once resolved) is reflected in these aggregate numbers, even though the specific report itself stays private.
