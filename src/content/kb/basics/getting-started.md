Omniplex lists three kinds of things: **bots**, **servers**, and **packs** (curated collections of bots). This guide covers signing in and submitting each one.

## 1. Sign in

Use the "Sign in" button in the header to authenticate with Discord. You'll need to be signed in before you can submit anything, vote, or manage your dashboard.

## 2. Submit a bot

Go to [Add a Bot](/bots/add). You'll need:

- **Bot ID** — your bot's Discord user/application ID, found in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications).
- **Short description** — up to 191 characters, shown on bot cards.
- **Invite URL** — the OAuth2 invite link for your bot.
- Optionally: prefix, library, tags, and a long description (HTML supported) for the bot's full page.

Before submitting, read [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) and [Page Rules](/kb/bots/page-rules) — bots are reviewed against both.

## 3. Submit a server

Go to [Add a Server](/servers/add). You'll need a Server ID, a short description, and a working invite URL. Read [Servers → Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules) first.

## 4. Create a pack

Go to [Add a Pack](/packs/add). Packs need a name, a unique URL, a short description, and 1–10 bots — but only bots you've already submitted yourself can be added to a pack. See [Packs → Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules).

## What happens after you submit

New bots and servers start in a **pending** review state. A member of our staff team will review the submission against the relevant rules before it's approved and made public. This isn't instant — please don't resubmit or open a support ticket asking for a status update shortly after submitting.

Once your listing is live, you can start collecting votes (see [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules)), and — if it qualifies — apply for [Certification](/kb/programs/certification) later on.
