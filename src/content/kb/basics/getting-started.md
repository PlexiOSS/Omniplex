Omniplex lists three kinds of things: **bots**, **servers**, and **packs** (curated collections of bots or servers, or sets of emojis, stickers or sounds you upload). This guide covers signing in and submitting each one.

## 1. Sign in

Use the "Sign in" button in the header to authenticate with Discord. You'll need to be signed in before you can submit anything, vote, or manage your dashboard.

## 2. Submit a bot

Go to [Add a Bot](/bots/add). You'll need:

- **Bot ID**: your bot's Discord application ID, found in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications).
- **Short description**: 30 to 150 characters, shown on bot cards and in search.
- **Long description**: at least 500 characters, shown on your bot's page. Markdown and a safe subset of HTML are supported.
- **Invite URL**: the OAuth2 invite link for your bot. It must use HTTPS and must not request the Administrator permission.
- **Prefix**: 1 to 10 characters. If your bot only uses slash commands, use `/`.
- **Library**: what your bot is built with, such as discord.js or disgo.
- **Tags**: 1 to 5 tags, each 3 to 30 characters.
- Optionally: extra links (website, support server, GitHub and so on) and a team to own the bot. See [Teams](/kb/listings/teams).

Before submitting, read [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) and [Page Rules](/kb/bots/page-rules). Bots are reviewed against both.

## 3. Submit a server

Go to [Add a Server](/servers/add) and paste an invite link. We look the server up and fill in its name, icon and member count for you. Our tracking bot needs to be in the server, and you need to own it or have Manage Server there. You then add a short description (30 to 150 characters), a long description (at least 500 characters) and 1 to 5 tags. Read [Servers → Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules) first, and see [Listing a Server](/kb/listings/listing-a-server) for how our tracking bot keeps your listing up to date.

## 4. Create a pack

Go to [Add a Pack](/packs/add) and pick a type first. Each type has its own flow:

- **Bot and server packs** bundle up to 10 listed bots or servers. They can include anyone's listings, not just your own.
- **Emoji and sticker packs** bundle up to 50 images you upload (256KB each at most).
- **Sound packs** bundle up to 50 MP3 clips you upload (2MB and 10 seconds each at most).

Every pack needs a name, a unique URL and a short description. See [Packs → Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules).

## What happens after you submit

New bots and servers start in a **pending** review state. A member of our staff team reviews the submission against the relevant rules before it's approved and made public. While it's pending, only you, your team and staff can see it. Review isn't instant, so please don't resubmit or open a support ticket asking for a status update shortly after submitting. You'll get a notification when a decision is made, with the reviewer's feedback.

Once your listing is live, you can start collecting votes (see [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules)). If it qualifies, you can apply for [Certification](/kb/programs/certification) later on.
