A pack groups things around a theme under one name and page. Anyone with an account can make one from [Create a Pack](/packs/add). Read the [Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules) first.

## Step 1: Choose what's in it

A pack holds one kind of thing:

| Type | What goes in it | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Bots | Bots listed on Omniplex, yours or anyone's | 10 |
| Servers | Servers listed on Omniplex, yours or anyone's | 10 |
| Emojis | Images you upload | 50 |
| Stickers | Images you upload | 50 |
| Sounds | MP3 clips you upload | 50 |

## Step 2: Details

- **Name**: 3 to 20 characters.
- **URL**: 3 to 20 letters, numbers or dashes, like `cozy-cats`. It's your pack's address, `omniplex.gg/packs/cozy-cats`, and can't start or end with a dash.
- **Short description**: 10 to 100 characters, shown on cards and in search.
- **Tags**: 1 to 5, so people can find the pack.

For emoji, sticker and sound packs, **choose the URL before you upload anything.** Uploads are stored under the URL, so it locks after your first upload. To change it, remove every item first.

## Step 3: Add the contents

- **Bot and server packs**: search for listings by name and choose **Add**. Your own bots also appear under **Quick-add your bots**.
- **Emoji, sticker and sound packs**: upload your files and give each one a name. See [Emoji and Sticker Packs](/kb/packs/emoji-and-sticker-packs) and [Sound Packs](/kb/packs/sound-packs) for formats and limits.

The preview beside the form shows how your pack will look. When you're happy, choose **Create Pack**.

## After creating it

Your pack is live straight away. You can edit or delete it from your [dashboard](/dashboard). See [Managing Your Packs](/kb/packs/managing-packs).
