Sound packs share short clips that people can preview, download and add to their server's soundboard.

## File format and limits

- **Format**: MP3 only.
- **Length**: up to 10 seconds per sound.
- **Size**: up to 2 MB per sound.
- **Per pack**: up to 50 sounds.

The length is checked when you pick a file, so a clip that's too long is rejected before it uploads.

## Making good sounds

- **Trim the silence** at the start and end, so the sound plays the moment someone clicks it.
- **Even out the volume.** A clip much louder than the rest of a soundboard gets muted fast.
- **Keep them short and recognisable.** The best soundboard sounds are a second or two.

## Adding them to your server's soundboard

1. Open the sound's page, play it to check it, and choose **Download**.
2. In Discord, open **Server Settings**, then **Soundboard**, and upload the file.

Discord's soundboard has its own, shorter limits on length and file size, so very long clips may need trimming before Discord accepts them.

## What's allowed

Only upload sounds you made or have permission to share. No copyrighted music clips you don't own, and nothing hateful or designed to startle or harm, like ear-rape or flashing-alert sounds. See [Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules).
