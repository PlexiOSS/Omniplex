---
title: Pack Rules
description: The rules that all bot packs on Omniplex must follow.
order: 1
---

A pack is a curated collection of your own bots, grouped together under a single name, short description, and page. Keep in mind that all of the rules below are just general guidelines we may offer exemptions or extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis.

1. A pack must contain between 1 and 10 bots, and only bots you have submitted yourself can be added to a pack.
2. Packs should group bots around a genuine, coherent theme (e.g. a suite of related bots, or bots that work together) rather than being an arbitrary or random grouping.
3. Pack names and short descriptions must follow the same content rules as [Page Rules](/kb/bots/page-rules) no spam, no NSFW mentions, no misleading information.
4. Do not create multiple near-duplicate packs solely to increase your visibility or vote count a pack should serve a real discovery purpose for users.
5. Every bot included in a pack must individually follow [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) a pack does not exempt any of its bots from those requirements.
6. Staff reserve the right to remove a pack, or remove individual bots from a pack, at any time if it stops meeting these rules.
