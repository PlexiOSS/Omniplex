Emoji and sticker packs let you share sets of images that anyone can download and add to their own Discord server.

## File formats and limits

| | Emojis | Stickers |
|---|---|---|
| Formats | PNG, WebP, JPEG, GIF | PNG, WebP, JPEG, GIF |
| Max size per file | 256 KB | 256 KB |
| Max per pack | 50 | 50 |
| Animated | Upload a GIF | Upload a GIF |

Animated items get an **Animated** badge on their page.

## Naming

Give every item a clear, unique name. Emoji names are what people type in Discord (`:party_cat:`), so keep them short and use letters, numbers and underscores. Prefixing names with your pack's theme (`cat_wave`, `cat_love`) avoids clashes with other emojis in a server.

## Making them look good in Discord

- **Use a transparent background** (PNG, WebP or GIF), so they look right in both light and dark mode.
- **Emojis**: square images at 128 × 128 work best. Discord shows them small inline and larger when sent on their own, so keep details bold.
- **Stickers**: square images at 320 × 320 work best.

Each emoji and sticker page has a **How it looks in Discord** preview showing it in a message, in both themes, so you can check before sharing.

## Adding them to your server

1. Open the emoji or sticker's page and choose **Download**.
2. In Discord, open **Server Settings**, then **Emoji** or **Stickers**.
3. Upload the file and give it a name.

Discord's own limits apply when you add them. For example, stickers must be PNG, APNG or GIF, and how many you can add depends on your server's boost level.

## What's allowed

Only upload images you made or have permission to share, and nothing NSFW, hateful or imitating Discord's branding. See [Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules).
