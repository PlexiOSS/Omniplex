A team lets several people manage the same bots and servers without sharing an account. Every member gets only the permissions you give them.

## Creating a team

Go to [Create a Team](/teams/add), give it a name and a short description, and create it. You're its owner. Servers always belong to a team: when you list a server without choosing one, a team is created for it automatically.

## Adding members

1. Open the team's page and go to its settings.
2. Under members, click **Add Member** and enter the person's Discord user ID. (Turn on Developer Mode in Discord, then right-click their name and choose "Copy User ID".)
3. Choose what they're allowed to do.

## Permissions

Permissions apply to the team and everything it owns. The main ones:

| Area | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Team** | Change the team's name, description and links; change vanity URLs. |
| **Bots** | Add or transfer bots in, edit their settings, delete them, resubmit denied ones, request certification. |
| **Servers** | The same for servers, plus reading and changing their invite. |
| **Members** | Invite people, change what members may do, remove members. |
| **Webhooks** | See, create, test and delete webhooks, and read or clear their delivery logs. |
| **Assets** | Upload and delete avatars, banners and other images. |
| **Reviews** | Write, edit and delete the team's owner replies to reviews. |
| **API tokens** | See, create and revoke [API tokens](/kb/developers/api-tokens). |
| **Shop** | Redeem vote credits and spend them in the [Shop](/kb/premium/shop). |

**Owner** includes every permission, including deleting the team. Each permission is described in full where you assign it.

## Moving a bot into a team

Open your [dashboard](/dashboard), open the bot's menu, and choose **Change Team**. You need to be able to delete the bot to move it, since moving it hands control to the team. You can also pick a team when you first submit a bot.

## Team pages

Every team has a public page listing its members and everything it owns. Teams can be voted for and reviewed like bots and servers.
