## Vanity URLs

A vanity URL replaces the ID in your listing's link: `omniplex.gg/bots/your-bot` instead of a long number. Set it from your [dashboard](/dashboard) when editing a bot or server.

- Vanity codes are lowercase, and spaces become hyphens.
- Only plain (ASCII) letters, numbers and symbols are kept, and `@` isn't allowed.
- Each code can only be used once across the whole site, and some words are blocked.

The old ID-based link keeps working after you set a vanity URL, so nothing you've already shared breaks.

## Banners

A banner is the wide image at the top of your listing's page and on its card.

- Upload one when editing your bot or server in the [dashboard](/dashboard). You need permission to manage assets if the listing belongs to a [team](/kb/listings/teams).
- WebP, PNG, JPEG or GIF, up to 5MB. A wide image around 3:1 looks best.
- A new upload shows up everywhere straight away.

If a bot has no uploaded banner, its Discord profile banner is used instead, when it has one. Without either, the page shows a short coloured strip.

## Avatars and names

Bot and user avatars and names come from Discord, so you change them there, not on Omniplex.

- We refresh Discord profiles in the background, bots first, so changes usually appear within a few hours.
- Images are mirrored through our own CDN, so an avatar can briefly show the old picture until the next refresh.
- Server icons and names are kept up to date by our tracking bot (see [Listing a Server](/kb/listings/listing-a-server)).
- Team avatars are uploaded on the team's settings page and update immediately.

If an avatar or name is still out of date after a day, [open a support ticket](/tickets) with the listing's link.
