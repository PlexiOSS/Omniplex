title: Page Rules
description: The rules that all bot and pack pages on Omniplex must follow.
order: 2
---

Keep in mind that all of the rules below are just general guidelines — we may offer exemptions or extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis.

1. You may not include spam/junk, or completely irrelevant/nonsensical characters or phrases in your bot description to intentionally use up the character limit.
2. You may not mention NSFW or include any NSFW content on the bot page (avatar or images).
3. You may not use your page maliciously against users by either providing or linking to malicious content.
4. You may not block any essential features, such as buttons on your page.
5. You should use "Extra Links" and other functionality to improve your page and bot rather than serving unrelated content.
6. You should aim to provide a good user experience on your page, and should not use it to intentionally annoy users.
7. You should aim to provide up-to-date information, such as an up-to-date invite and prefix, and you should not try to mislead users.
