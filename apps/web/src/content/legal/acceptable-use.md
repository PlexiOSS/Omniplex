This covers conduct across Omniplex generally. Listing-specific rules live in [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) and [Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules): this page is the rest, how you're expected to behave using the site itself.

## Not allowed

- **Harassment or abuse** of other users, listing owners, or staff, on Omniplex or through it.
- **Impersonation** of another user, listing, or of Omniplex staff.
- **Vote manipulation**: alt accounts, vote-selling, vote-for-vote arrangements, or any other attempt to fake support for a listing. See [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules) for how voting works and what counts as abuse.
- **Spam**, including repeated low-effort listings, reports, or support tickets meant to waste staff time rather than raise a real issue.
- **Filing false reports**: reporting a listing for a reason you know doesn't apply, to get a competitor delisted or to harass someone.
- **Payment fraud**, including chargebacks on purchases you actually received, or attempting to exploit the shop or premium system.
- **Bypassing enforcement**, such as creating a new account to get around a ban, vote ban, or listing suspension.

## Reporting a violation

Any bot, server, or pack has a "Report this" option on its listing page. Pick the reason that fits and describe what's wrong. For issues that aren't tied to a specific listing, like harassment or account-level abuse, [open a support ticket](/tickets) instead.

## What happens when this is broken

Depending on severity, staff can warn, suspend, vote-ban, delist, or ban. Repeated violations are treated more seriously than a first-time issue. We publish aggregate numbers on how reports are handled at [Moderation Transparency](/kb/basics/moderation-transparency); individual reports stay private, but the outcomes are reflected in those totals.

If you think an enforcement action against you was a mistake, [open a support ticket](/tickets) and staff will review it.
