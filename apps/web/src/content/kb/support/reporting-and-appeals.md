## Reporting a listing

If a bot, server, team or pack breaks our rules, report it so staff can take a look:

1. Open its page and click **Report**, near the top.
2. Pick a reason: **License violation**, **Terms of Service violation**, **Spam**, or **Other**.
3. Describe what's wrong in 10 to 500 characters. Be specific: which command, which part of the description, what you saw and when.
4. Submit. You need to be signed in.

Useful things to include: a command name, a message link, or the exact text that breaks the rules. "This bot steals tokens" is hard to act on; "`/verify` asks users to paste their Discord token" is not.

## What happens next

Staff review every report. Your identity is never shown to the person you reported. Resolving a report and acting on the listing are separate steps for staff, so a listing can stay up while a report is being looked into, and a report can be closed without action if nothing breaks the rules.

You won't get a personal reply for every report, but the outcomes are reflected in the aggregate numbers on [Moderation Transparency](/about/moderation).

For anything urgent, or something that isn't about a listing (a user harassing you, a payment problem), [open a support ticket](/tickets) instead. See [Support Tickets](/kb/support/tickets).

## Appealing a ban

If your Omniplex account is banned, signing in takes you to the ban page instead of the rest of the site. From there you can sign in with the ban appeal option and submit an appeal explaining why you think the ban was a mistake.

- You can only have one appeal open at a time, and you'll see its status (Pending Review, Approved or Denied) on the same page, along with any feedback from staff.
- You don't need to submit another appeal while one is pending.

A **vote ban** is different: it only stops you from voting. If you think you were vote banned by mistake, [open a support ticket](/tickets).

Bans from our Discord server are covered by the [Community Policies](/kb/basics/community-policies).
