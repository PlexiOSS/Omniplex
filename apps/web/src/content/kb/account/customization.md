Open **Customize** from the sliders icon in the top bar (on phones, it's in the menu) to change how Omniplex looks and what it shows you.

## Theme

**Presets** set the whole look in one click: colours, font, corners and spacing, and for some of them page width, text size, and light or dark mode. You can change anything afterwards.

**Surface** changes the greys behind everything, separately from the accent colour. **Accent** picks the colour used for links and buttons.

Some presets, like Discord and Midnight, switch to dark mode, and Paper switches to light mode. Those show a moon or sun icon.

## Layout

- **Page width**: how wide pages can get.
- **Corners**: sharp, default or round.
- **Density**: how much space there is between things.
- **Text size**: scales text and spacing together.
- **Motion**: follows your device's setting by default. **Reduce** turns off animations and smooth scrolling.

## Content

- **Card stats**: which numbers show on bot and server cards.
- **Hidden tags**: bots and servers with any of these tags are left out wherever cards are listed, such as browse, search and the home page. You can hide up to 50 tags.
- **Hide offline bots**: leaves out bots we've seen offline. Bots whose status we can't see still show.
- **NSFW**: hide NSFW listings, or blur their images.

These filters only hide cards. A page you link to directly still opens, and pages of results may look shorter than usual because hidden cards are removed after they load.

## Saving your settings

If you're signed in, your settings are saved to your account and follow you to other devices. If you're signed out, they stay on this device. The bottom of the panel shows which applies.

**Reset to defaults** puts everything back, including on your account.
