Premium gives one of your bots or servers a shorter vote cooldown and a Premium badge on its card and page.

## What premium changes

- **Vote cooldown** drops from the standard 12 hours down to 4 hours (see [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules) for the full breakdown, including weekend bonuses).
- A **Premium badge** appears on its card and detail page.

## Plans

Go to [Premium](/premium), pick **Bot** or **Server**, then pick the listing you want to upgrade. Only your own approved or certified listings that aren't already premium are eligible. Plans are Bronze, Silver, and Gold, differing only in how many days of premium you get for the price.

## Paying

Two ways to pay:

- **Card**, via Stripe Checkout. You're sent to Stripe's hosted checkout page, then back to Omniplex once it's done.
- **PayPal**, which works the same way: you're sent to PayPal and back.

Neither method has your card or PayPal details ever touch Omniplex directly. After a successful payment you'll land on a confirmation page, and premium is applied automatically with no separate step needed. If a card payment went through but premium hasn't appeared, give it an hour: we check for paid purchases that weren't delivered every hour and apply them. If it still isn't there after that, [open a support ticket](/tickets).

## Using credits for a discount

Bots and servers earn vote credits from their votes (see [Vote Credits & the Shop](/kb/premium/shop)). At checkout you can spend some of the listing's credits for money off:

- Every **2 credits** take **1 cent** off the price.
- Credits can cover up to **half** of the plan's price. The rest is paid by card or PayPal.
- The order summary on the [Premium](/premium) page shows how many credits you can use and the discount they give. Tick the **Use … credits for … off** option before choosing how to pay.

You need permission to spend the listing's credits, the same as in the shop. The credits are taken when you start checkout. If you don't finish paying, they're returned to the listing automatically within a few hours, and you get a notification when they are.

## Discord server boosters

If you boost *our* official Discord server (not the same thing as a "server" listing; this is about being a Nitro booster of our community), you get a free Bronze-plan redemption once every 30 days instead of paying. This option appears automatically on the [Premium](/premium) page when you're eligible.

## Prefer not to pay with money?

Bots and servers both earn a spendable credit balance from the votes they receive. Besides discounting a plan at checkout, credits can buy bonus premium days in the shop. See [Vote Credits & the Shop](/kb/premium/shop).
