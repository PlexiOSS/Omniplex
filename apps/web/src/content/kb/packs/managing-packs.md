## Editing a pack

Open the **Packs** tab in your [dashboard](/dashboard). From there you can change a pack's name, description and tags, and add or remove its contents. For emoji, sticker and sound packs you can also rename items and give them a short link.

## Sharing a pack

- **Short link**: copy it from the buttons under the pack's description.
- **Embed**: the **Embed** button gives you a widget image for a website or README. See [Widgets](/kb/developers/widgets).
- **Individual items**: every emoji, sticker and sound has its own page you can link to directly.

## Votes

People can vote for packs just like bots and servers. Votes count toward the pack's place in listings and reset monthly, and pack votes count on the [voter leaderboard](/leaderboard). See [Voting Rules & FAQ](/kb/voting/voting-rules).

## Deleting a pack

Delete a pack from the **Packs** tab in your dashboard. This removes the pack and all of its items from Omniplex, including every emoji, sticker or sound page in it. It can't be undone.

## Reporting a pack

If a pack breaks the [Pack Rules](/kb/packs/pack-rules), for example by sharing someone else's work without permission, use **Report** on the pack's page.
