---
title: Pack Rules
description: The rules every pack on Omniplex must follow, whether it bundles bots, servers, emojis, stickers or sounds.
order: 1
---

A pack is a curated collection under a single name, short description and page. **Bot and server packs** bundle up to 10 listed bots or servers. They can include anyone's listings, not just your own, so a pack like "Best Music Bots" is welcome. **Emoji, sticker and sound packs** bundle up to 50 items you upload yourself.

Keep in mind that all of the rules below are just general guidelines. We may offer exemptions or extra restrictions on a case-by-case basis.

## Every pack

1. A pack should group its contents around a genuine, coherent theme (a suite of related bots, the best servers for a game, a set of emojis in one style) rather than being an arbitrary or random grouping.
2. Pack names and short descriptions must follow the same content rules as [Page Rules](/kb/bots/page-rules): no spam, no NSFW mentions, no misleading information.
3. Don't create multiple near-duplicate packs just to increase your visibility or vote count. A pack should help people discover something.
4. Don't present a pack of other people's listings as if you made them or speak for them.

## Bot and server packs

5. Every bot or server in a pack must individually follow [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) or [Listing Rules](/kb/servers/listing-rules). Being in a pack doesn't exempt anything from those rules, and a pack built around rule-breaking listings (account generators, for example) will be removed.

## Emoji, sticker and sound packs

6. Only upload things you made or have the right to share. Packs built from someone else's work without permission will be removed if reported.
7. No NSFW, hateful or shock content, and nothing that imitates Discord's own branding or staff badges.

## Enforcement

8. Staff reserve the right to remove a pack, or remove individual items from a pack, at any time if it stops meeting these rules.
