Reviews let you tell other people what a bot, server or team is actually like to use. Every review has a star rating and some text.

## Writing a review

1. Open the listing's page and go to the **Reviews** tab.
2. Click **Write a review**, pick 1 to 5 stars, and write between 5 and 4,000 characters.
3. Post it. You need to be signed in.

You can write one review per listing, and only approved or certified listings can be reviewed. You can edit or delete your own review at any time from the same tab.

## Replies

You can reply to a review to add context or answer a question. Replies can be nested up to three levels deep.

## Owner responses

A listing's owner, and team members with permission to manage owner reviews, can respond on the listing's behalf. These are marked **Owner response**, so it's clear they come from the people behind the listing. To post one, click **Reply** on a review and leave **Post as an owner response** ticked. It's the best way to respond to a complaint publicly, and a good owner response often does more for a listing than the original review.

## What's not allowed

Reviews follow the same rules as everything else on the site (see [Community Policies](/kb/basics/community-policies)):

- No spam, advertising, or reviews that have nothing to do with the listing.
- No harassment, hate speech or NSFW content.
- No fake reviews, and no reviews written to boost your own listing or attack a competitor.

If a review breaks these rules, report the listing it's on with a link to the review (see [Reporting & Appeals](/kb/support/reporting-and-appeals)).

## Where reviews appear

A listing's average rating and review count are shown on its Reviews tab. Your own recent reviews also appear on your profile's **Activity** tab.
