The views on your bot or server's card and page are the number of visits in the last 30 days.

## How views are counted

- **One visitor, once a day.** Each visitor counts once per listing per day, however many times they reload. People sharing an internet connection, like a school or office, can count as one.
- **Real visits only.** A view is recorded by the visitor's browser after the page opens. Link previews, widgets and known crawlers don't count.
- **Some visits are missed.** Visitors with JavaScript off, or who block the request, aren't counted. Treat views as a good estimate, not an exact figure.

**Invite clicks** work the same way: each visitor counts once per listing per day when they open your invite.

## Your privacy

We don't use cookies for this. To spot repeat visits we keep a scrambled code in place of the visitor's IP address, using a secret that changes daily. It's deleted automatically within two days, and only the daily totals are kept.

## Why the numbers changed

In November 2026 we rebuilt view tracking. The old system had stopped counting visits from the website, and its older totals were inflated, with many listings showing tens of thousands of views from only a few dozen visitors. Everyone's 30-day views started from zero. Your dashboard's Statistics section still shows an all-time total, which includes those older numbers.

**Most Viewed** on the home page ranks by the last 30 days, and uses all-time totals only to break ties.

You can hide your views and invite clicks under **Public stats** in your listing's Details settings.
