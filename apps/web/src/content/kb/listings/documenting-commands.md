Documenting your bot's commands gives people a reason to pick it before they've even invited it. Documented commands show on your bot's page under the **Commands** tab, and in the [Command Directory](/commands), where anyone can search every bot's commands at once.

## Adding commands

1. Open your bot's page and go to the **Commands** tab.
2. Click **Edit Commands**. You'll see this if you own the bot, or if you're on its team with permission to edit its settings.
3. Add a row for each command:
   - **Name**: the command itself, such as `/help` or `ban`. A leading `/` is added for display if you leave it out.
   - **Usage** (optional): how to call it, such as `/ban <user> [reason]`. Only shown when it adds something beyond the name.
   - **Description** (optional): what it does, in a sentence or two.
   - **Category** (optional): commands with the same category are grouped together, such as "Moderation" or "Fun".
4. Click **Save**.

## Limits

- Up to 100 commands per bot.
- Names up to 100 characters, usage up to 200, descriptions up to 500 and categories up to 50.

## In the Command Directory

Once your bot is approved, its commands are searchable in the [Command Directory](/commands) by name and description, and people can filter by your bot or by its tags. The directory also links back to your bot's page, and your bot's Commands tab links to it.

A few good descriptions go a long way. "Bans a member and optionally deletes their recent messages" is far more likely to be found than "ban cmd".
