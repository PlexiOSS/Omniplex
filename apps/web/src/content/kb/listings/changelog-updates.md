Your bot's **Changelog** tab is where you announce new features, fixes and other news. Posting updates shows your bot is actively maintained, and every update gets your bot in front of people browsing the site.

## Posting an update

1. Open your bot's page and go to the **Changelog** tab.
2. Click **Post Update**. You'll see this if you own the bot, or if you're on its team with permission to edit its settings.
3. Give it a **title** (up to 200 characters), an optional **version** such as `2.1.0` (up to 50), and the **content** (up to 10,000 characters).
4. Post it.

You can delete an update afterwards from the same tab.

## Formatting

Updates support Markdown: headings, **bold** and *italic* text, lists, links, `inline code` and code blocks, plus a safe subset of HTML. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. A single line break inside a paragraph is joined into the same line, so text you've wrapped in your editor (or copied from a `CHANGELOG.md`) still reads as one paragraph.

## Where updates appear

- **Your bot's page**, under the Changelog tab, newest first. Each update has its own link, so you can share one directly.
- **The [feed](/feed)**, which collects updates from every listed bot. People can search it and filter by tag or by bot.
- **The homepage**, where the "Recently updated" list shows the bots that posted most recently.

Only updates from approved and certified bots appear in the feed and on the homepage.
