Every bot is reviewed by a member of staff before it appears on Omniplex. Knowing what they check makes approval much more likely.

## How review works

1. Your bot joins the review queue as soon as you submit it.
2. A reviewer claims it, invites it to our testing server and tries its commands.
3. They approve or deny it, and you get a notification either way. A denial always comes with the reason.

Bots are reviewed in the order they were submitted. How long that takes depends on how busy the queue is, so please don't resubmit or ask staff to skip ahead.

## What reviewers check

- **It's online and responds.** Keep your bot running while it's in the queue. An offline bot is denied.
- **It has a clear way in**, like a working help command, and at least 7 working commands (unless it serves one designated purpose).
- **Nothing is broken.** Every command a user can find should work.
- **Permissions are reasonable.** It doesn't need Administrator, and each command only asks for what it uses.
- **Owner commands are locked.** Eval, status and similar commands must only work for you.
- **It follows the rules.** See [Bot Rules](/kb/bots/rules) for the full list, including bans on YouTube music playback, account generators and invite rewards.
- **The page is accurate.** The description matches what the bot does, and follows the [Page Rules](/kb/bots/page-rules).

## If your bot is denied

Read the reason in the notification, fix what it mentions, then submit the bot again from [Add a Bot](/bots/add). A denied bot can be resubmitted with the same application ID.

If you think the denial was a mistake, you can ask for it to be looked at again from the [Apply](/apps) page.

## After approval

Your bot goes live straight away. From there you can [document your commands](/kb/listings/documenting-commands), [post changelog updates](/kb/listings/changelog-updates), [post stats](/kb/developers/posting-stats) and [set up vote webhooks](/kb/developers/vote-webhooks).
