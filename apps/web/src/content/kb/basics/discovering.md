There's more than one way to find the right bot or server. Here's what each part of the site is good for.

## Browse

[Bots](/bots) and [Servers](/servers) list everything that's approved. Sort by:

- **Newest**: recently listed first (boosted listings appear at the top).
- **Top voted**: the most votes this month.
- **Trending**: the most votes in the last 7 days.
- **Most servers** (bots) or **Most members** (servers).

Narrow it down with the tag chips, or switch on **Certified only** to see listings staff have certified. Your filters are kept in the link, so you can bookmark or share them.

## Search

[Search](/search) looks for bots, servers, teams and packs by name, description and tags. If you know what you want by name, this is the fastest way there.

## Tags and collections

Every tag has its own page (for example [Moderation](/tags/Moderation)) with everything that uses it and related tags to explore next. [Collections](/collections) are ready-made best-of lists, like the best music or moderation bots, ranked by votes.

## The Command Directory

The [Command Directory](/commands) searches the documented commands of every bot at once. Looking for something with a `/giveaway` command? Search for it there and compare bots side by side.

## The feed

The [feed](/feed) shows changelog updates from every bot, newest first. It's a good way to spot bots that are actively developed, and you can search it or filter by tag or bot.

## Packs

[Packs](/packs) are curated bundles of bots or servers put together by the community, plus emoji, sticker and sound packs you can use in your own server.

## Comparing

On any bot or server page, **Compare** puts it side by side with another, with votes, servers or members, and tags.

## Voters and leaderboards

The [Voter Leaderboard](/leaderboard) shows the community's most active voters, and every bot or server has a **Voters** tab showing who votes for it most.
