Omniplex is a Discord bot and server listing platform. This site covers
the Popplio API, building integrations against it, and the practical
guides for listing and managing a bot or server. For everything else,
sign in, submitting a listing, rules, and site policy, see the
[knowledge base](https://omniplex.gg/kb) on the main site.

## FAQ

### Can I self-host Omniplex?

No, self-hosting is not officially supported.

### My bot/server was denied or removed. What do I do?

Check the reason given on the listing or in your notifications. If you
believe it was a mistake, reach out through the site's support channels.

### Where do I report a bug or ask for help?

Join [our Discord server](https://discord.gg/zmQE9TZF9J) and ask in
support. There's no separate support email.
